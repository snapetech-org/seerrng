## SeerrOrig general profile settings

SeerrOrig now follows upstream's profile form order and language option labels. SeerrNG-only language preferences, streaming subscriptions, additional media request limits, extra watchlist automation, card settings, category visibility, and profile integrations are available in collapsed extension sections. The SeerrNG theme keeps its existing expanded controls.

The SeerrNG logo and brand assets remain in use. This aligns the shared profile presentation with Seerr v3.5.1 while preserving the fork's settings and behavior.

Source: [Seerr v3.5.1](https://github.com/seerr-team/seerr/commit/564bfb08b54caa374bddaf820abfd226e5e69151) by [@gauthier-th](https://github.com/gauthier-th). They contributed the source to upstream Seerr; SeerrNG independently adapted the form presentation and preserved its setting behavior.
