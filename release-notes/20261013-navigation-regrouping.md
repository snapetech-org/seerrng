The main menu now groups discovery choices and library pages in a clearer order, and places User List under Settings. Movies, series, books, and audiobook discovery pages now offer a Discover/Swipe switch, including their genre, keyword, language, studio/network, and upcoming views. Returning from Swipe restores the page and filters you left.
