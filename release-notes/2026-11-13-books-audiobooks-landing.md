Books and Audiobooks now open on separate discovery pages with In your library, Trending, New releases, Top rated, and Popular rows. Genre and sort selections open a catalogue grid and stay in the URL. Open Library covers use SeerrNG's image cache, audiobooks without art borrow a matching Hardcover cover, and books without a confirmed library match no longer show an unknown-status badge.
