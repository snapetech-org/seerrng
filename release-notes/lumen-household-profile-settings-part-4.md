Local account owners can upload a JPEG, PNG, or WebP profile picture from My Profile. The signed-in account picture also appears in Lumen navigation and refreshes after an upload. Recent Requests on household profiles shows each request’s status before opening its history. Pending requests over quota show the same Over Quota label in household previews and on the Requests page.
