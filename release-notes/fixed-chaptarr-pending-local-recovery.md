Pending Chaptarr requests can reconnect to a local book when an older Goodreads identity no longer resolves. Recovery matches saved title, author, work, format, and edition without loading the author's catalog or creating a duplicate. Tracked work IDs keep their provider identity when format-scoped lookups return bare IDs. Ambiguous matches and active imports or searches remain untouched.
