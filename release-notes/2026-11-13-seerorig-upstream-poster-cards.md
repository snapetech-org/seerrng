SeerrOrig movie, TV, and collection posters now follow Seerr's upstream card framing, hover shadow, image overlay, typography, and status presentation. SeerrNG's own logo, fork-specific poster actions, and extra media information remain available.

Source: [upstream Seerr commit 564bfb08](https://github.com/seerr-team/seerr/commit/564bfb08b54caa374bddaf820abfd226e5e69151), authored by [@gauthier-th](https://github.com/gauthier-th), who contributed the original card presentation to upstream Seerr. SeerrNG independently adapted it; this does not imply direct contribution to or endorsement of SeerrNG.
