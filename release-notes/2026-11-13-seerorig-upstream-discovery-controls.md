SeerrOrig now uses upstream Seerr's original Trending controls and error presentation on movie and series discovery pages. SeerrNG's multi-catalog trending tabs remain available in its native interface.

SeerrNG independently adapted these presentation behaviors; this does not imply direct contribution to or endorsement of SeerrNG.
