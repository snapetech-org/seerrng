Dropdowns and split-button menus in SeerrOrig now use Seerr's upstream menu sizing, surfaces, transitions, and item layout. SeerrNG-only action variants remain available and use the closest upstream presentation; the SeerrNG theme keeps its existing menu style.

Source: [upstream Seerr commit 564bfb08](https://github.com/seerr-team/seerr/commit/564bfb08b54caa374bddaf820abfd226e5e69151), authored by [@gauthier-th](https://github.com/gauthier-th), who contributed the original change to upstream Seerr. SeerrNG independently adapted the menu presentation; this attribution does not imply direct contribution to or endorsement of SeerrNG.
