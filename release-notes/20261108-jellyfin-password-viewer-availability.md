Jellyfin and Emby password sign-in retains the viewer credential for availability and Watch links. Restricted libraries stay hidden. Existing password sessions without a saved viewer credential must sign out and sign back in through Jellyfin or Emby once. Lumen and the full interface now show reconnect instructions for affected accounts.
