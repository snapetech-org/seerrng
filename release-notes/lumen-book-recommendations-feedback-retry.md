- Opening a recommendation feed updates the URL so it can be bookmarked and restored on reload.
- Recommendation bookmarks also restore the selected ebook or audiobook format.
- Lumen opens a newly created recommendation feed and keeps the form available for retry if Hardcover rejects creation.

- Bookshelf matches opened from a recommendation keep their edition format and title lookup hint.
- Switching between ebook and audiobook details preserves the Bookshelf lookup title.
- Opening a Bookshelf result from Share preserves the resolved title and edition format.
- Bookshelf cards in search and discovery carry the title needed to open the matching edition.

Book Series catalog links also retain the matched Bookshelf title and selected format.

Release Calendar links preserve the Bookshelf title for local book releases.

Request links carry the resolved Bookshelf title and format, including when Lumen restores a cached row.

Switching formats from an older Bookshelf bookmark carries forward the title resolved on the detail page.

Bundled Lumen redirects older padded Bookshelf bookmarks to the book and keeps the title and format.
