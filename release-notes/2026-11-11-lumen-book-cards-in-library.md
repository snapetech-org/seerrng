Book cards in Lumen show a check for books already in your library on the Books and Audiobooks pages, Discover and search. Cards label unresolved Open Library matches as unknown, and the Not yet filter hides those items instead of treating them as unowned. The filter also explains when a library check fails or exceeds its bounded scan.
