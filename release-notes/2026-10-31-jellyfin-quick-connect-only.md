Administrators can require Jellyfin Quick Connect by hiding the username and password form and rejecting password-based Jellyfin sign-in requests. Local account and OpenID Connect sign-in remain independently configurable. Requested in Seerr [issue #3300](https://github.com/seerr-team/seerr/issues/3300).
