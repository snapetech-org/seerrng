With Aurral or Lidarr connected, Lumen's Music section gets Discover, one search for artists, albums and songs, artist and album pages with previews and one-tap Get, a filterable Library, and Activity that explains failed downloads. On Lidarr, the library, activity and follows come from Lidarr, and charts, genres and previews from Deezer.
