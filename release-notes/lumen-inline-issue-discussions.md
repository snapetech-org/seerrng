Lumen issue reports can now expand inline to show the discussion and available actions without leaving the filtered issue list. Source: [upstream issue-management work](https://github.com/seerr-team/seerr/commit/e402c42aaa7d795cd724856a2e23615bb1a3695d), contributed to the upstream project by [@sct](https://github.com/sct). SeerrNG independently adapted this interaction for Lumen's discussion workflow and permissions.
