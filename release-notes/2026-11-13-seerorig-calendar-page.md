The SeerrNG release calendar now uses SeerrOrig's upstream page heading, notices, controls, links, cards, and status badges while retaining the calendar's cross-media data.

Source: [upstream Seerr commit 564bfb08](https://github.com/seerr-team/seerr/commit/564bfb08b54caa374bddaf820abfd226e5e69151), authored by [@gauthier-th](https://github.com/gauthier-th), who contributed the shared presentation patterns to upstream Seerr. SeerrNG independently adapted those roles for its calendar; this does not imply direct contribution to or endorsement of SeerrNG.
