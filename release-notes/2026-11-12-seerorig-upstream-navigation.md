SeerrOrig now keeps Seerr's Discover, Movies, Series, Requests, Blocklist, Issues, Users, and Settings links in upstream order. SeerrNG-only destinations remain available under a collapsible More section. The header and Discover home follow Seerr's upstream composition and default palette, the sign-in page and sidebar reuse Seerr's original logo assets, and the settings navigation follows the upstream tab set and spacing. Fork-specific settings remain accessible under More settings.
