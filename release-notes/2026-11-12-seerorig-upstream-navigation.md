SeerrOrig now keeps Seerr's Discover, Movies, Series, Requests, Blocklist, Issues, Users, and Settings links in upstream order. SeerrNG-only destinations remain available under a collapsible More section. The header and Discover home follow Seerr's upstream composition and default palette, while SeerrNG's existing branding stays in place. The settings navigation follows the upstream tab set and spacing, with fork-specific settings accessible under More settings. Search now matches upstream's results layout, and the Requests page uses upstream's primary filter controls; fork categories and advanced filters remain available under More.
