# Media Details and Playback

Media detail pages show availability and request actions alongside title
information. Some pages also include controls for playback quality and for
keeping useful information sections open.

Movie and Series availability can reflect the signed-in user's linked
media-server account. See [per-user movie and TV availability](./media-server-access.md)
for how restricted seasons, unverified items, and alternate request
destinations are represented.

## Keep detail sections open

Use the pin segment beside a disclosure control such as **View Cast**, **View
Crew**, **View Artists**, or **Subject Tags** to keep that section open on
future detail pages in the same media category. Use the main control to open or
close a section on the current page.

Pinned sections are saved to your SeerrNG user settings and follow your account
across navigation and devices. Movie, Series, Music, and Book pins are
separate; pinning one category does not open the same section for another.
Collections use the Movie category's pins, and Book Genres uses the Book
category's Subject Tags pin.

## Hide subject tags

Movie and Series Subject Tags panels include a visibility button beside the
heading. Use it to hide or show the keyword links when they reveal plot details.
The choice applies to both Movie and Series pages and is saved in this browser
for the current SeerrNG account. It does not sync to another browser or device.

## Choose what to play

When playback controls are available, use the quality selector before
playback. Movie and Series pages offer the available video quality, such as HD
or 4K; Music pages offer MP3 or FLAC. The playback action uses the selected
quality. For episodes or tracks, use the selection circles to choose specific
items; with no items selected, playback uses all available items in that
selection.

**Play on Device** creates a temporary playback queue. **Play on your media
server** opens a persistent SeerrNG playlist named **SeerrNG - Current
Selection**, replacing that playlist's previous contents. Playback uses only
the selected quality, so separately cataloged quality versions are not mixed.
